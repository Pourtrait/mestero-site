3. Information we collect

Our games do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We collect the following:

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, app version, and crash and performance data.
  • Identifiers: a device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS when you allow tracking), an app-specific installation ID, and your IP address, from which an approximate country or region can be derived.
  • Gameplay data: levels played and completed, progress, coins and items, settings, session length, and how you interact with features and ads.
  • Advertising and attribution data: which ads you saw or interacted with, and which ad campaign, if any, led you to install the game.

Information you provide

  • Player name: if you choose a player name for leaderboards, it is stored with your score and shown to other players. Please don't use your real name or anything that identifies you.
  • Support messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple. We receive a confirmation of what was bought and when. Our purchase service, RevenueCat, receives the store's purchase confirmation and an anonymous app user ID so purchases can be validated and restored on a new device. We never see or store your card or payment details.

4. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and run leaderboards.
  • To deliver purchases and restore them on a new device.
  • To fix bugs, measure performance and improve levels and features.
  • To show ads, including personalised ads where you have allowed them, and to cap how often you see them.
  • To understand which marketing campaigns bring new players, so we can spend our marketing budget sensibly.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating and abuse, and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

5. Advertising

Our free games are funded by ads. Ads are delivered through Google AdMob, which runs an auction between a number of ad networks. These partners may use your device's advertising identifier and other device information to show ads, measure them and, where permitted, personalise them.

  • UK, EU and EEA players: we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game's settings.
  • iOS players: we only access your IDFA if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt. You can change this in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Android players: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings under Privacy, Ads.

If you decline personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

7. Legal bases (UK and EU)

  • Contract: to provide the game, save progress and deliver purchases.
  • Consent: for personalised advertising and, where required, for advertising identifiers and attribution.
  • Legitimate interests: for analytics, crash reporting, fraud prevention and non-personalised ads, where our interests are not overridden by yours.
  • Legal obligation: to keep records required by law, such as tax records for purchases.

8. Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU and UK, where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Where a game identifies a player as a child, we turn off personalised ads and limit data collection to what the game needs to work.

9. How long we keep information

We keep gameplay and leaderboard data while you continue to play, and delete or anonymise it after 24 months of inactivity. Analytics data is kept in aggregated or pseudonymised form for up to 26 months. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Partners keep data according to their own policies.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email privacy@mestero.com and include the game's name and, if possible, the player ID shown in the game's settings so we can find your data. We reply within one month. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), and in the EU to your local data protection authority.

11. Deleting your data

To delete your game data, including your player name, scores and saved progress:

  1. Email privacy@mestero.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us the game's name and your player ID (found in the game's settings).

We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. Uninstalling the game removes data stored on your device, but not data held on our servers, so please send the request if you want everything removed. Purchases made through Google Play or Apple remain on record with those stores.

12. International transfers

Our partners may process information outside the UK and EU, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or adequacy decisions.

13. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and reputable service providers to protect information. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

14. California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. Showing personalised ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state laws. You can opt out by declining personalised ads in the game's privacy settings, by limiting ad tracking on your device, or by emailing us. We will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

15. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when we add games or partners, or when the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make significant changes, we will tell you in the game.
